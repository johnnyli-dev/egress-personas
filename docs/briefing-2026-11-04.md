## The mechanism, if they ask how it works

> The literature keeps the shape of the population; the persona decides who is early and
> who is late.

A persona could be handed an absolute pre-movement time. That would throw away
Lovreglio's Weibull, fitted to 149 and 78 observations, and substitute a guess. So
instead:

1. compose a score from weighted persona traits — milling, seeking a second cue,
   compliance, training, commitments, whether they can hear the alarm;
2. rank that score within the population;
3. read the value off the published fit at that quantile.

| model input | published mean | produced |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-movement time | 119.8 s | 119.3 s |
| Queue patience | 60.0 s | 60.0 s |
| Smoke turn-back visibility | 3.252 m | 3.252 m |

### Volunteer the limitation before they find it

That check validates the **plumbing, not the weights**. Ranks are uniform by
construction, so the published distribution comes out right for *any* scoring function —
including one with the signs reversed. The weights are `ESTIMATE`: each sign is
defensible from NIST's cues → milling → actions → delay model, the magnitudes are
reasoned. Only a sensitivity run, or observed orderings, can test them.

## If asked "so what does this change about the evacuation?"

**We don't know yet, and that is the next piece of work.** The pipeline moves *inputs* to
RSET — a slower left tail, fewer residents anybody would warn, more people who cannot use
the stairs. Turning that into "this building takes four more minutes" needs two things:

- the population loaded into the simulation (its own reserved roadmap item; a contained
  piece of Rust — a struct of optional floats, a branch in the spawn function, a scenario
  key);
- the sensitivity run.

**Do not let a cohort comparison get read as a finding.** Every cohort target is
`basis = team` — a number somebody chose in order to ask a question with it. The
comparison shows the arithmetic consequence of those choices propagated through the
model's own rules. The rows worth a second look are the ones where a consequence turns up
in a quantity the cohort never named: the elderly cohort doubles the walking-difficulty
share without setting one, because mobility is conditioned on age.
